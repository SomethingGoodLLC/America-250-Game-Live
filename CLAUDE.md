# CLAUDE.md — Play America 250 (code name Samson)

An original Unreal Engine 5.8 game. C++ for the authoritative simulation, Blueprints/UMG for gameplay and UI, developed on Apple Silicon.

## Locked decisions
- Simulation is authoritative and deterministic; UI, audio and video are not.
- Campaign state lives in the PA250Sim C++ core (`unreal/PA250Sim`), which has no UObject or Unreal headers. A `UGameInstanceSubsystem` exposes it to Unreal.
- Pure functions: `advance_hour`, `enqueue_command`, `plan_path`, `apply_battle_result`, `validate_proposal`, `apply_diplomacy`, `register_diplomacy_failure`; since 2026-11-14 also `split_field_strength`, `rounds_per_man_for`, `evaluate_outcome`, `mission_predicate`, `reenlist_companies`, `send_for_siege_guns`, `attach_siege_guns`, `choose_ai_target` and `auto_resolve_battle` (tests and `pa250sim_tool` only; the game never auto-resolves); from the battle pass of 2026-11-14 also `split_fallen`, `send_stores_to_depot`, `served_guns` and `choose_battle_mode`.
- Campaign pass (2026-11-15) pure functions: `halt_army`, `march_plan`, `validate_campaign_order`, `apply_campaign_order`, `commander_fate`; `purchase_cost_now` reads the price rise.
- Determinism: seeds come from CampaignState, never the wall clock. Containers that affect results are ordered. Sim advances in fixed 1-hour ticks; UE speed only changes how many ticks are requested per real second.
- State-changing diplomacy clauses, only after validation:
 - `ceasefire(days)` (stored as hours_remaining = days*24)
 - `prisoner_exchange(counts)`
 - `safe_passage(army_id, province_id, days)`
 - `cede_province(province_id)`
- `defect` (GAME INVENTION, British envoy only): the envoy turns traitor when the player's real offer (title + gold/10 + 15 per granted province) meets the sim's price; it must be the only clause (title `mayor_of_boston`). Boston changes hands in three ways only (user decision 2026-11-14): `defect`; the evacuation (siege guns on fortified Dorchester start a countdown, cancelled if the British retake the heights); or a siege after a won battle there (72 h per fort level). `cede_province` of Boston stays forbidden.
- `surrender(army_id, days)` (GAME INVENTION, British): honors of war after Fort Necessity 1754. A cornered army (enemy strength in/beside its province >= 1.5x) marches out unharmed, yields the ground, and is paroled up to 30 days. Never inside a protected province.
- `vassalage` (GAME INVENTION, British envoy offers, the player must accept): gold uncapped, never Boston as a fief; the player becomes the Crown's vassal under a permanent peace.
- `take_offense(reason, days 1-7)` (GAME INVENTION): a slighted envoy refuses all clauses for those days; grievance and attitude persist in FactionInfo. `register_diplomacy_failure` is the pure function for talks that end without agreement.
- The spoken line is display-only and never parsed for state.
- British red lines for the slice: never cede Boston; ceasefire 1–365 days and safe_passage 1–90 days (GAME INVENTION); prisoner counts no higher than held.
- Old dialogue intents (trade, alliance, peace, war, resource_sharing, concession, counter_offer, ultimatum, small_talk) stay non-authoritative. Do not rewrite `protocol/schemas/proposal.yaml`; add new schemas beside it.
- Negotiation service: use only `services/negotiation/main.py` (`uvicorn main:app`). Leave `app/main.py` and `SessionManager._generate_outcome` unconnected.
- Avatar: file-per-line MP4 behind `BaseVideoSource`, emitting `sadtalker_video_ready`. Chain: musetalk_local → musetalk_remote (not built) → fal_talking (`veed/fabric-1.0` 480p default; never the deprecated `fal-ai/musetalk`) → D-ID (our WAV via `/audios`, never D-ID's voice) → audio-only, all under `PA250_AVATAR_TIMEOUT`. Voice is ElevenLabs `pcm_24000` WAV played through `USoundWaveProcedural`, sent as soon as TTS finishes (voice first; a rendered MP4 is used only from cache, or with `PA250_VIDEO_WAIT=1`). The envoy is a living man seated across the table, never a framed picture (user decision 2026-11-10): a real period portrait of the person (Gage: Copley, CC0, `SourceArt/envoy/gage_copley_original.jpg`) is made photoreal and cut out by `tools/art/build_envoy_live.py`, and shown on the plane tagged `Portrait`. Live lip-sync: `M_PortraitWarp` deforms that figure from the service's 30 Hz viseme curves; face landmarks live in `FACE_BRITISH` (`build_levels.py`) / `DA_EnvoyFace_british`. The old painted flipbook atlas and the stock MP4s (rendered from the retired painted face; `PA250_STOCK_VIDEO=1` to offer them) are not used. MediaPlayer is for MP4 only, and the WAV and MP4 never play together. No WebRTC inside Unreal. No live frame streaming.
- Weapon: `RFCharacter` fires `AMusketWeapon` (projectile). Hitscan is retired for battles.
- Soldier classes are `Army.units` keys: `line_infantry`, `militia`, `rifleman`, `officer` (Continental); `line_infantry`, `grenadier`, `light_infantry`, `officer` (British); and for both sides the support classes `sapper`, `ensign`, `musician`, `surgeon`, `artilleryman` (battle pass 2026-11-14). Support classes are raised, never given at the start, and the British AI can raise them (`british_ai.recruit_targets`; a gun crew only while a gun lacks one). Companies are raised through `start_recruitment` (catalog `recruits` in `buildings_catalog.json`: `faction`, `requires_chapter`). Battle kit numbers (health, speed, aim, range, reload, bayonet, officer bonus, preferred range, verb values) live in `unreal/PA250Sim/data/unit_classes.json` and are served only through `Battle/PA250UnitClasses` (user decision 2026-11-14). Its C++ table is a fallback: any use logs an `Error` (`PA250 unit classes: FALLBACK`) and `RunClasses` fails if the file is missing or lacks a class. Bot drill stays in `DA_CombatProfiles`. The class picker offers only classes the army holds. Class looks (height, headgear, coat) are in `ARFCharacter::ApplyClassLook`; card images are in-game stills (`-PA250ClassPortraits` → `tools/art/build_class_portraits.py` → `Scripts/import_class_portraits.py`), re-shot whenever a look changes.
- Player input is a `player_utterance`, typed or spoken: Speak/Stop records the mic, `POST /v1/stt` (local faster-whisper) transcribes it, and the text is sent like typed words.
- The Godot tree under `engine/` is retired. Do not extend or port its apply logic.
- No multiplayer networking, databases, brokers, or extra services.
- Battles change campaign state only through `BattleResult`. Every state change is a command (order or result); mission rewards and failures are `CommandKind::MissionEffect`.
- Campaign orders (2026-11-15): `protocol/schemas/campaign_orders.yaml`, vectors in `protocol/test_vectors/campaign`. Orders: `halt`, `form_army`, `assign_person`, `relieve_person`, `use_agent`, `lobby`, `vote_now`, `send_dispatch`, `decide`. Halt is `CommandKind::HaltArmy`; the rest are `CommandKind::CampaignOrder` (the order object in `result_json`), queued through `CampaignController::issue_order` and applied on the next hour. While the clock is stopped for a pending battle or a pending decision the controller applies the order at that hour boundary instead, as it does a battle result.
- Halt: an army can be between towns (`Army.province` is the town its leg began in, `path` the edges ahead, `hours_into_edge` the hours spent). Halted at rest the order and progress are cleared; halted part-way it turns about (`returning_edge`, `returning_hours`) and needs the march hours it had spent to be back, and starts no new march until then.
- People (GAME INVENTION for every number and every trait; `persons_boston_1775.json`, `protocol/schemas/persons.yaml`): one role at a time: general, delegate, organizer, envoy, agent. An army with no general is a detachment: it takes in at most `detachment_unit_cap` units and cannot besiege; `form_army` gives a free general an army standing in a town the side holds that has a muster (a Militia Muster or a Fort), and is refused while the army has a general (relieve him first). No army opens as a detachment (user decision 2026-11-15): Colonel James Barrett, who commanded at Concord on 19 April, has the opening militia (`starts_as`); Heath is at hand from that day. Starting roles are seeded by `load_scenario`, so they stand before the clock has run. The AI follows the same rule at midnight. Generals have two traits from the closed set Defensive, Aggressive, Steadfast, Adroit, Engineer, Popular; a trait is a `factors[]` row with the general as its source. Agents spend uses: alarm (on the pre-battle card, defender only), scout, engineer, boats, pen. The enemy column on the pre-battle card is a rough count unless the player has scouted that army, holds an organizer in the town, or has an informer in the enemy's councils (Benjamin Church gives the British that until 4 October 1775). Every claim about a person is marked `documented` or `invented`.
- Lobbying (GAME INVENTION; `bodies_boston_1775.json`, `measures_boston_1775.json`, `protocol/schemas/politics.yaml`, effects closed set `effects.yaml`): a body has three blocs with a lean; a measure passes when `blocs_to_pass` blocs vote for it, each drawn from the campaign seed. The Massachusetts Provincial Congress sits until 10 May 1775, then the Continental Congress; the British answer to the King's ministers, whose measures are dispatches (38 days out, 38 back, in data). Favors come from missions, letters and victories. Unity is 100 less the spread between the blocs; under its floor units from other colonies (`Army.allied`) go home one a day. The ministry's confidence falls with British defeats; under its floor the commander in chief is recalled and the next general at Boston takes the command and the parley.
- Decisions (GAME INVENTION; `decisions_boston_1775.json`, `protocol/schemas/decisions.yaml`): exactly two options, each with a cost, a gain and a delayed consequence; an option may commit a person, who leaves the map for a set time. A pending decision stops the clock (`Interrupt::Decision`) until it is answered. The other side's commander takes `ai_option` without stopping it.
- Letters (2026-11-15): every answer except set aside costs or gains something, printed on its button (`letters_have_consequences` walks every letter and fails on one that does not). Choice effects are data (`opening_war_correspondence.json`, `protocol/schemas/correspondence_effects.yaml`), including favors, one battle factor at a named town, units held from the field (`hold_units`), a donor reserve's powder (`donor_transfer`), stores moved at once (`provisions_now`) and the offensive put off. No letter keeps a number or a branch of its own in the sim (user decision 2026-11-15: L3, L5 and L7 did until then; `letter_numbers_are_data` loads a changed catalog and checks the sim follows it). The detached commander's letter is the `commander_letter` block: its factors (`withdraw_early`, `stand_fast`, `magazine_saved`, `morale`) stand at Concord until the next battle there.
- Battle stories (2026-11-15; `battle_stories.json`, `protocol/schemas/battle_stories.yaml`, display only): "To arms!" shows the story (picture with a slow pan, title, date, history, the player's situation, credit) while the battle level loads behind it (`LoadPackageAsync`); Continue hands the picture to the engine's loading screen (`MoviePlayer`) for the level change, and the field fades in. Pictures are public-domain works, none shipped: `PA250StoryArt` shows a generated wash until `/Game/PA250/Stories/T_Story_<key>` is imported. Narration is pre-rendered (`tools/audio/gen_story_narration.py`, `Scripts/import_story_audio.py`) and played only if imported.
- The campaign pass runs only while its data files are loaded (the game loads them all; a missing or refused file is an `Error`). `CampaignState.pass` is saved only once it holds something, so a campaign without the files saves as before.
- Battle contract: `protocol/schemas/battle_setup.yaml` and `battle_result.yaml`, vectors in `protocol/test_vectors/battle`. The setup carries per-side `field_strength`, `rounds_per_man`, `fortification_level` and `factors[]` (label, value, source); the result carries `end_reason` (`tickets_exhausted`, `time_limit`, `force_preserved`, `routed`, `army_broke`, `posts_swept`) and `prisoners`. The winner takes the town for every end reason except `force_preserved`; a fort turns the loss into a siege. `army_broke` and `posts_swept` can beat the player's side while he is alive. Since 2026-11-14 the setup also carries `mode` (`conquest`, `assault`, `landing`, chosen by the sim's `choose_battle_mode`) and per-side `guns`; the result carries `casualties` in men (killed, wounded, prisoners by class; the unit fields are legacy input), `colours_captured` and `guns_taken`.
- Since 2026-11-15 a setup side may carry `commander` (id, name, traits, aura radius), `alarmed` and, on the enemy's side only, `intel` (a rough count of men for the card; the battle always uses the true numbers); factor labels gained `commander`, `alarm`, `withdraw_early`, `stand_fast`, `magazine_saved`. The result carries `commander_events` (faction to `wounded`, `killed` or `captured`). In the battle the general is an officer-class figure who keeps near his side's home post, is no part of the tickets or the casualty ledger, and whose fall is a morale check on his side. Campaign morale for the armies that fought is `battle_rules.json` `morale.victory` and `morale.defeat` (+5 and −20; an `army_broke` loss uses `army_broke.morale_penalty`). A fallen general still makes his army a detachment; the morale hit is `army_morale_on_fall` when his side lost and `army_morale_on_fall_victory` (0) when it won, so a victory does not go negative from the fall alone (locked 2026-11-15).
- Men, not units (user decision 2026-11-14): a battle reports men; `Army.men_down` carries the remainder until a whole unit is gone. Men under arms keep their arms: no one is stood down for what the town's magazine holds, and `field_strength` never exceeds the men fielded.
- Field guns (GAME INVENTION, user decision 2026-11-14): `Army.guns`, `ProvinceStores.guns`. One `artilleryman` unit serves one gun; an army brings the guns it has crews for and draws guns from the magazine of a town its side holds. Concord opens with 2 (count invented), the Boston garrison with 4 and 4 crews; a column detached from the garrison takes its share; Knox's train gives 2 and 2 crews once. Only the winner keeps guns it overran. The purchased cannon (`Content/ThirdParty/PTM_Cannon`, one mesh) wears the project's `M_PA250_Cannon`; its values are `battle_rules.json` `cannon`.
- Battle modes (GAME INVENTION): landing (the attacker came by a `boat` or `ferry` edge into a `harbor` town; fought on the generated `L_Harbor`, first wave in `APA250RowBoat`), assault (the defender behind works: posts carried in order, the clock is the defender's), else conquest; the North Bridge and Battle Road presets are always conquest. The `boston-dorchester` boat edge (user decision 2026-11-14) makes a British attack on the heights a landing.
- Battle levels are generated (`Scripts/build_levels.py`) and carry markers the battle reads: `PA250WorksSlot` (+ `redoubt`), `PA250GunSlot`, `PA250BoatLanding`; `L_Harbor` tags starts by role (`PA250Attacker`, `PA250Defender`). Before a level is regenerated (user decision 2026-11-14): back up the `.umap` files to `Saved/PA250/level_backups/<date-time>/`, dump the saved level (`Scripts/dump_level_actors.py`), build it under a scratch name (`PA250_LEVEL_SUFFIX=_regen`), compare (`tools/ue/compare_level_dumps.py`), and stop if the saved level holds anything the generator does not produce.
- Bot budget (user decision 2026-11-14): `field.bot_budget` rises only if 60 bots hold `field.frame_rate_bar` (45 fps) in 3 samples of 3 at the resolution the game is normally played at. Measured 2026-11-14 at 1710x1112: 16 to 19 fps; it stays 40.
- Carried rounds (user decision 2026-11-14): every army saves the rounds in its men's cartridge boxes (`Army.rounds_per_man`; `-1` is a full box, read it through `carried_rounds`). The boxes pay for what the army fires; a side fighting in a town it holds is fed by that town's magazine first. A beaten army that quits the field drops `defeat_drop_pct` of its boxes (`rout_drop_pct` for `routed` and `army_broke`) and the winner's men fill up from it. Boxes fill only while the army stands in a town its side holds, charged to that magazine (`refill_cartridge_boxes`, hourly clock rule). A column never draws on the town it marched from.
- Campaign arc (`src/campaign.cpp`): outcome, missions, enlistments, sieges, evacuation and the British AI run only when `campaign_rules_boston_1775.json` and `missions_boston_1775.json` are loaded. Mission predicates and effects are closed sets (`protocol/schemas/missions.yaml`).
- Tuning is data, never C++ constants (smoke, sound, the shove, modes, guns, works and casualties included): `battle_rules.json`, `campaign_rules_boston_1775.json`, `missions_boston_1775.json`, `unit_classes.json`, `buildings_catalog.json`, `logistics_tuning.json`, and since 2026-11-15 `persons_boston_1775.json`, `bodies_boston_1775.json`, `measures_boston_1775.json`, `decisions_boston_1775.json`, `battle_stories.json` and the choice effects in `opening_war_correspondence.json` (all in `unreal/PA250Sim/data`).
- Buildings answer to the ground (`requires_tags`, `income_requires_tags` against `Province.terrain`): Powder Mill needs river or falls, Farm needs farmland, Depot trade needs harbor or crossroads, powder is bought only through a Depot in a harbor town. A building that stood before the rules is listed in `Province.grandfathered` and keeps working. Levels cost ×1, ×2.5, ×6, upkeep in step. Every building effect must show in `factors[]` or the monthly ledger (`building_effects_have_outlets`).
- Golden files (`protocol/test_vectors/clock_golden_1775.json`): regenerate only for a deliberate hash change, after every other case passes, and log the cause with the old and new hash in `PROGRESS.md`. Never regenerate to clear an unexplained failure.
- Persistence is one JSON save of CampaignState at an hour boundary (schema_version 2).

## Unreal on this Mac
- The HUD is scaled by the window's height (the engine's default DPI curve), so its width in Slate units depends on the aspect, not the size: 1280x720 and 1920x1080 are 1920 units wide, the usual play size 1710x1112 is 1661. A layout that fits a 1280x720 still can be over-full at the play size (the top bar's Support box was squeezed to nothing there until 2026-11-15). Check HUD changes at both: `RESX=1710 RESY=1112 OUT_DIR=<dir> scripts/run_campaign_probe.sh PA250PassProbe …` logs `top bar FITS` or `DOES NOT FIT` with each piece's width.
- Top bar (user decision 2026-11-15): one row of treasury, stores, Support, the Congress (or Whitehall) button with Unity and favors, People and the date; the score line and the Ledger sit on a strip under the date. The title plaque shows only on a bar at least 1800 units wide.
- Verify in a real window: editor binary, `-game -windowed`, Metal. Take screenshots after at least 10 s, never at frame 5. `HighResShot` omits Slate UI; use `Shot showui` for HUD checks.
- `-ExecutePythonScript` is refused in `-game`; use `-ExecCmds="py <script>"`. A probe can set the cursor and call a click handler for scripted clicks.
- Unlit map materials need fixed exposure (manual exposure, tone curve 0, no bloom), or colors wash out or darken.
- Perspective cameras use the engine default `AspectRatio_MaintainYFOV`: FOV is horizontal only at 16:9.
- Textures: confirm mips were generated (non-power-of-two sizes may get none). `tools/ue/build_map_assets.py` `_import_texture` always disables mips.
- Python scripts that need Pillow run with `/opt/homebrew/bin/python3`.
- Never write a skeletal mesh's bones from game code (no `const_cast` on `GetComponentSpaceTransforms()`, no editing bone arrays in an actor or component tick). It races the parallel animation task and crashes skinning with `Assertion failed: IsRotationNormalized()` in `UpdateRefToLocalMatricesInner`. Bone edits (IK, aim) go inside pose evaluation: `FPA250SoldierAnimProxy` in `RF/PA250SoldierAnimInstance.cpp`. Game code only publishes targets.
- Mouse/trackpad look only gets deltas while the game viewport holds mouse capture. PIE starts without it (`GameGetsMouseControl=False`). Keep `ARFCharacter::EnsureGameMouseCapture`; never assume the player clicked the viewport (they may fire with F). If look is dead, check the log for `PA250 input:`.
- Build with the editor closed. With it open, UBT writes `libUnrealEditor-PlayAmerica250-NNNN.dylib`, which the running editor does not load, so playtests still run the old code. Before blaming a crash on new code, compare the editor's start time with the dylib's timestamp.
- Commandlets run `-nullrhi`, so GPU skinning (where bone asserts fire) never runs there. A bone-safety fix is verified only in a rendered PIE or `-game` run.
- A `-game` run started from a script without `-windowed` (the saved windowed-fullscreen mode) stops at window creation on this Mac; scripted runs use `-windowed -ResX -ResY`, and those values are written into `Saved/Config/MacEditor/GameUserSettings.ini`.
- Powder smoke hides men from the bots and is simulated headless too (`-PA250NoSmokeSight` for an A/B run).

## Procedural Blender assets and Unreal import

- Generate actual editable geometry with Blender Python (`bpy`), not image mockups or pseudocode. Inspect the reference and available tools first. Treat illustrations as visual references, not architectural blueprints; use plausible historical construction and dimensions in meters.
- Use configurable dimensions and a fixed random seed, reusable geometry/material helpers, named collections, separate decorative meshes, applied transforms and outward normals. Author Z-up with a ground-level building-center origin; document the forward axis (the farmhouse uses −Y). Keep silhouette and joinery detail useful at tactical distance without inflating polygon counts for Nanite.
- Deliver the generator, config, `.blend`, `.glb`, `.fbx` when supported, texture PNGs and front/side/isometric renders. Keep the source pack separate from generated Unreal `.uasset` files. Export only asset meshes; exclude preview lights, cameras and ground.
- Prefer Blender background execution: `blender -b --factory-startup --python generate_farmhouse.py -- --output .`. If the Blender app is unavailable, the **official `bpy==4.5.3` wheel with Python 3.11** is a tested alternative on this Apple Silicon Mac: create a dedicated venv, install that package, and run `generate_farmhouse.py` with the venv's Python. This runs the actual Blender API, exports and Cycles renderer. The original `/private/tmp/pa250-blender-python` environment is temporary; do not assume it persists. Report what was actually executed.
- Blender-only procedural shader nodes do not automatically survive export. Generate or bake portable PBR image maps, use export-supported Principled materials, pack textures into the blend and embed them in GLB. Base color is sRGB; roughness and normal maps are linear. Avoid double-converting sRGB pixels when writing generated byte images. In Unreal, directly imported OpenGL +Y normal maps need **Flip Green Channel** enabled and normal-map compression. UV0 may tile for surface materials; baked lighting needs a separate non-overlapping lightmap channel.
- Validate by reopening the saved blend and re-importing both GLB and FBX. Check object/triangle counts, metric bounds, transforms, normals/topology, UVs, embedded textures and collision names. Inspect actual renders and record the results; successful export alone is not validation.
- **Working reference pack:** `/Users/leone/Documents/ChatGPT/Play America 250/ColonialFarmhouse` (outside this repository). Verify it exists before reuse. `generate_farmhouse.py` builds the asset, `validate_farmhouse.py` performs Blender round-trip checks, and `import_into_unreal.py` imports and verifies the Unreal assets. Its `config.json` controls farmhouse dimensions, seed and optional props. Reuse its helpers for new building recipes rather than assuming it already generates churches, barns or arbitrary floor plans.
- Prefer automated Unreal import for generated asset packs. With `PythonScriptPlugin` and `EditorScriptingUtilities` enabled, run `UnrealEditor-Cmd <project.uproject> -run=pythonscript -script=<importer.py> -unattended -nullrhi -nosplash`. The tested importer uses `AssetImportTask`, explicit PBR material graphs and a compiled Actor Blueprint with aligned static-mesh components. It does not save levels. Protect unrelated assets: use a dedicated destination and ownership metadata, and refuse to overwrite assets not owned by the importer.
- Export separate FBXs per collision-bearing static mesh, with convex hulls named `UCX_<exact render mesh name>_##`. Import at scale 1 with scene/unit conversion enabled, Combine Meshes off, automatic collision generation off and One Convex Hull per UCX on. GLB is portable visual interchange; do not rely on it to carry UE collision semantics. The reference importer selects legacy FBX through the process-local `Interchange.FeatureFlags.Import.FBX 0` console variable; do not change persistent project settings for this.
- Verify imported assets in a **fresh Unreal process**. The reference importer supports `PA250_FARMHOUSE_VERIFY_ONLY=1`. Check dimensions in centimeters, triangle counts, material graph connections, texture settings, saved collision hulls and compiled Blueprint component transforms/profiles. `StaticMeshEditorSubsystem` may be unavailable in a commandlet: inspect `StaticMesh.body_setup.agg_geom.convex_elems` directly. UE 5.8 subobject gathering can return duplicate handles; deduplicate by the component UObject path, not by mesh name.
- The first farmhouse is imported at `/Game/PA250/Buildings/ColonialFarmhouse`: `BP_ColonialFarmhouse`, 4 meshes, 13 materials and 33 textures (51 assets). UE 5.8.4 fresh-process validation passed with 6 house and 13 fence collision hulls. It is an exterior-only asset with opaque tinted glazing. Headless validation does not establish rendered appearance, gameplay collision behavior or GPU correctness; use the real-window verification rules above for those.

## Generated images (OpenAI)

## Map pipeline (`ports/GrandStrategyMac`, TestHost only)
- The purchased InteractiveMap plugin is read-only. Fixes go in `TestHost/Source/PortHost`; map assets go under `/Game/<MapName>`. The repository must stay private (`port-changes.patch` holds plugin source).
- Lookup texture: exact unique RGB per region, never filtered. Import as UserInterface (`TC_EditorIcon`), no mips, sRGB off, nearest, never stream; anything else fails silently (white map).
- MapData JSON keys match the USTRUCT fields exactly, case-sensitive: `ID`, `Name`, `Area`, `Region`, `StructType`. A mismatch gives `NO NAME` with no error.
- On Metal the plugin's GPU recolor renders black and its political materials sample whole-Earth textures. Use a project-local unlit material: display texture plus lookup-matched highlight.
- `UMapObject::PostLoad` (editor) needs the lookup texture preloaded, or the level crashes on load. `PortHost.cpp` preloads only the colonies texture by path; generalize it before adding a second map.
- The sample HUD's debug key Q crashes on custom maps.
