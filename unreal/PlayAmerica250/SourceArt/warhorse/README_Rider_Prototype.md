# Mounted rider prototype

`/Game/PA250/Characters/WarHorse/Rider/A_Rider_MountedIdle` is a four-second
prototype for `/Game/Characters/Mannequins/Meshes/SK_Mannequin`, generated from
`MM_Idle` by `Scripts/build_mounted_idle.py`. It includes a seated leg blockout,
arm adjustments and gentle idle movement. The root is lowered about 94 cm so
the mannequin pelvis is near the attachment origin.

For preview, attach a mannequin skeletal mesh component to the horse mesh at
`rider_seat`, assign this animation and loop it; adjust its relative rotation and
position to fit the saddle. The original horse idle and its other clips remain
separate and can play on the horse at the same time.

This is procedural blocking. Inspect and adjust leg placement, reins, saddle
contact, rider orientation, loop continuity and deformation in the editor before
using it in gameplay. The horse animation import also reported bind-pose warnings.
The rider is not yet wired to a pawn, and this does not include a slash animation.

## Colonial soldier and slash

`APA250MountedSoldier` is a placeable C++ actor using the existing American
soldier mesh, the warhorse and a simple three-piece sword prop. On play the horse
and rider idle; call its Blueprint-callable `Slash` function to play one right-arm
cut and return to idle. The rider is attached to `rider_seat`, and the sword parts
are attached to `hand_r`. Component transforms are editable for fitting.

The soldier's own skeleton uses `A_Continental_MountedIdle_V2` (4.05 s) and
`A_Continental_MountedSlash_V2` (1.22 s), generated by
`Scripts/build_mounted_soldier.py`. The slash and idle are procedural blockouts.
The sword is a placeholder, and the actor is not connected to player input,
melee hit detection, or damage. Preview the poses, grip, horse-seat alignment
and animation transitions in the editor before integrating them in gameplay.

## Playable test mode

After the C++ project builds, start Play in the editor, open the console with
`~`, and enter:

```
open /Game/ThirdPerson/Lvl_ThirdPerson?game=/Script/PlayAmerica250.PA250MountedTestGameMode
```

Click the game viewport to capture the mouse. WASD moves, mouse looks, and E or
left click plays the slash. The horse switches between its idle and walk clips.
Escape stops Play in the editor. This is a separate test mode; it does not add
cavalry to the battle class selector or implement sword damage. Pose, grip,
collision and movement require a visual/playtest pass.

The V2 clips correct the right leg direction. The mounted actor also corrects
the saddle bone's rotation; transform checks confirm the rider remains upright
through sampled idle and walk frames. Check the result visually for saddle,
reins, sword grip, and body fit after rebuilding the project.
